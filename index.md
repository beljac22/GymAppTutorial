# How to use
This is an app meant to be as simple as possible while still helping users track workouts. I made it based on my own time going to the gym. Many alternatives have bloatware, adware, required signup, information collecting and more. This app does the bare minimum of helping users store and analyze gym information.

## General Use
1. Clear any previous lifts
2. Click the Log Lift button
3. Observe the statistics page

 ## How do I enter a lift?
 1. Enter the name of the lift
 2. Enter the muscle group
 3. Enter the number of sets
 4. Enter the number of reps
 5. Enter the timing of the exersise

## How do I clear my lifts?
1. Go on the lifts screen
2. Press clear lifts
3. Confirm
